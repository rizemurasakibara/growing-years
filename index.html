A little about me

I’m a motion and graphics designer, an intermediate front-end web designer, and a student journalist working as a layout designer for online desktop publishing. I like making ideas visible — through movement, images, and the web.

Chapter 01

My childhood

The first small moments

“It wasn’t all easy, but it wasn’t all bad either.”

I wasn’t always able to enjoy things as a kid. There were things that seemed more important than simply being a child. It wasn’t the hardest childhood, but it wasn’t the easiest or happiest one either.

One memory I still carry is riding a bike for the first time while I was with my mother’s side of the family. It was a simple moment, but it felt like a new kind of freedom.

Chapter 02

My growing-up
years

I watched myself change: from a kid who knew very little and had to learn to handle pressure, into someone who often excelled at the things he tried.

Being good at almost anything felt great, but it could be confusing too. I began to understand that finishing first or learning quickly didn’t make me better than anyone else.

Chapter 03

My life today

Creative work, new challenges

I’m officially an adult, but I’m still Robin — just changed by everything I’ve learned so far.

I started going to the gym at 17. At 16, I began earning from my skills as a video editor for small content creators. Now I edit videos, take commissions to create websites, and work through problems in Java and JavaScript.

I’m also continuing to grow as a motion and graphics designer and student journalist. Designing layouts for online desktop publishing lets me bring creativity and communication together.

Chapter 04

What shaped
me

My experiences shaped who I am now. The people I’ve welcomed into my life have influenced me in good ways. I’ve learned through losing people, keeping people, choosing the right people, confronting hard things, and facing consequences.

Accountability and patience are still lessons I practice. I’m proud of what I’ve learned, and I hope to keep becoming a better person.

Chapter 05 The next chapter

Keep learning.
Keep becoming.

I want to keep improving my creative and technical skills, take on work that challenges me, and become someone who treats people well while pursuing his goals.

One step at a time: stay curious, be patient with the process, own my choices, and make room for other people to grow too.
